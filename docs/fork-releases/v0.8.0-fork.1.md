# v0.8.0-fork.1 — v0.8.0 with this fork's player extras, and a fix for late-starting audio
PlxNative **v0.8.0** plus this fork's changes, built from [`claude/plx-native-version-diff-9faa0w`](https://github.com/danzig666/plx-native/tree/claude/plx-native-version-diff-9faa0w) by the fork's Sideload build workflow on the project's own toolchain.

## New since v0.7.0-fork.1

**Everything in upstream v0.8.0**: collections, Spanish and Belarusian, styled ASS/SSA subtitles, subtitle timing, size and position, subtitle search, Boost Dialog and Normalize Loudness, a persistent artwork cache, the Next episode and Skip interval settings, a Continue Watching setting, plaintext on the home network with consent, and many performance fixes. See the [upstream v0.8.0 release](https://github.com/GLinnik21/plx-native/releases/tag/v0.8.0).

**Fixed: a film whose audio starts late buffered forever at the start.** Some files (seen on a WEB-DL whose E-AC-3 track has an 11-second delay) carry no audio for the first seconds. The app's video buffer filled up before the first audio frame was reached, while the TV waited for audio, so the start never played; resuming past that point worked. The video buffer may now run further ahead until the first audio frame arrives.

## This fork's features, now on v0.8.0

- **Skip intros** and **Skip credits**: two settings under Settings → **Playback**, each *Show button* (the default) or *Skip automatically*. With *Skip automatically*, an intro, or credits in the middle of an item, is jumped past as soon as it starts, and an **"Intro skipped"** / **"Credits skipped"** notice appears at the top of the screen for a couple of seconds. At the closing credits of an episode the next one starts at once when the **Next episode** setting is *Up Next countdown*; with *After credits* or *Off* that setting decides, as before. Translated into Spanish and Belarusian.
- **BACK with the controls showing hides them**; BACK again leaves playback. While a video is still loading, BACK leaves straight away.
- **Magic Remote jitter no longer brings the controls back** after BACK hid them. The pointer has to move clearly (about 120 px) before they reappear.
- **Player controls follow the subtitle color**, one step brighter than the caption, so on an HDR picture the whole overlay is dimmed together. White and Silver leave the controls at full brightness.
- **Media line**: next to the line above the title, e.g. `4K · Dolby Vision · HEVC · TrueHD Atmos 7.1`, `1080p · H.264 · DD+ 5.1`, or `Converting · H.264 · AAC` on a transcode.

Not verified on a television yet: host unit tests and the ARM cross-build only.

## Installing

- `com.beb.plxnative_0.8.0_arm.ipk` installs **over** an existing PlxNative and keeps its sign-in. A later Homebrew Channel update to a newer upstream version will replace it.
- `com.beb.plxnative.debug_0.8.0_arm.ipk` installs **beside** it as a separate tile with its own sign-in (and a developer frame counter).

Install with webOS Dev Manager like any `.ipk`. Checksums are in `SHA256SUMS.txt`. The stable package reports no telemetry: no telemetry endpoint is built in.

The bundled FFmpeg is built by the same `ci/build-ffmpeg.sh` as upstream v0.8.0. Its LGPL corresponding source is the source asset on the [upstream v0.8.0 release](https://github.com/GLinnik21/plx-native/releases/tag/v0.8.0).
