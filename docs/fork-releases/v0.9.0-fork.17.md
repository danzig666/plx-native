# v0.9.0-fork.17 — upstream of 2026-10-29, numbered 0.9.17
PlxNative from upstream's main branch as of 2026-10-29 (everything in [upstream v0.9.0](https://github.com/GLinnik21/plx-native/releases/tag/v0.9.0)) plus this fork's changes, built from [`claude/plx-native-version-diff-9faa0w`](https://github.com/danzig666/plx-native/tree/claude/plx-native-version-diff-9faa0w) by the fork's Sideload build workflow.

## New in this build

**Upstream main of 2026-10-29** merged in (23 changes since fork.16), among them:

- **Audio the app decodes itself (FLAC, ALAC, PCM) plays in step with the picture:** it is fed later, by an amount that follows the sound output and LG's audio stack.
- **Hi-res stereo FLAC, ALAC and LPCM** (96 kHz, 192 kHz) are resampled by the app to 48 or 44.1 kHz instead of being converted to lossy audio by the server.
- **Force Direct Play, and a play whose audio the TV refused,** decode lossy audio in the app, DTS included; surround is no longer mixed down to stereo by default.
- **Faster pages:** the detail page, the start of playback and Home ask the server for what they need at the same time.
- **A new play never waits on the previous one closing,** which could freeze the screen.
- A crash when the app exits is fixed, and crash reports say more.
- A library page opened after the server changed its listing reloads it.
- **Polish** translation (this fork's Skip intros / Skip credits settings are translated too).

This fork's own changes are all kept, including the Blu-ray seeking fix from fork.16 and the player clock.

## From fork.16

- **Seeking works in Blu-ray remuxes.** In some films (Brink of War was the one reported) a seek, or a resume part-way in, sat on "Buffering…" for good. Such a file has only one "IDR" picture, its very first; every later point a seek can land on is a different kind of keyframe, which the player did not recognise, so it waited for a picture that never came. It now starts from those too.

## From fork.15


**Upstream main of 2026-10-26** merged in (60 changes since fork.14), among them:

- **Playback speed** from 0.5x to 2x, in the player.
- **Play Version:** pick which version of a movie or episode plays.
- **Previous and Next episode buttons** in the player, tap seeking with FF/REW, and an opt-in **Instant D-pad seeking** setting (Settings > Playback).
- **More audio plays directly:** FLAC, ALAC and plain PCM are decoded in the app, Opus plays directly, and audio above 48 kHz is converted when the TV cannot take it.
- **A video the TV refuses before its first picture is rescued:** it is converted under a 1080p limit and the refused version is not offered again; the app remembers which audio formats the TV really refuses.
- **Russian** translation (this fork's Skip intros / Skip credits settings are translated too).

This fork's own changes are all kept, including the player clock from fork.14 (the time of day in the top-right corner while the controls are up).

## From fork.14

- **A clock in the player.** While the player controls are on screen, the time of day shows in the top-right corner (for example 21:05, or 9:05 PM where the TV is set to a 12-hour clock). It goes away with the controls. It reads the TV's own time zone; if the time shown is off by whole hours, the TV's zone is not reaching the app, so please say so.

## From fork.13

**Upstream main of 2026-10-24** merged in (17 changes since fork.12), among them:

- **New and removed movies and shows appear without a restart:** when your server adds or deletes items, Home's Recently Added, the library shelves and the library grid refresh (the grid you are looking at waits until you leave it, so nothing moves under you).
- **BACK on a video that never starts:** the first BACK hides the controls, the second leaves. This is upstream's version of what the fork did with one press; it no longer ends a film by accident when you only meant to clear the controls after a seek or a rebuffer.
- A network fix: a finished download is read as finished instead of waiting on the open connection.

This fork's Magic Remote changes from fork.6 to fork.12 are all kept:

- From fork.12: **the top and bottom scrolling zones are smaller** (80 px instead of 120 px), so the page no longer starts gliding while you are only moving the pointer toward the top or bottom row. The left and right zones are unchanged.
- From fork.11: **Search results scroll at the same speed as everything else with the pointer.** Resting the pointer at the top or bottom edge of Search moved the results very slowly (about a seventh of the speed on other pages). A notch of the remote's scroll wheel still moves the list as before.
- From fork.10: **Rows scroll sideways with the pointer too.** Rest the Magic Remote pointer near the left or right edge of the screen over a row that does not fit (a Home shelf, a Recommended shelf, Search results, a person's films, and on a detail page Related, the collection, Extras, Cast and the episodes) and that row glides sideways, smoothly, faster the closer you are to the edge. It stays where you leave it until you move along it with the arrow keys or click one of its posters.
- From fork.9: **Detail pages no longer jump around while the pointer scrolls them.** On a series (or movie) page, every piece of the page that loaded during an edge glide (the episodes, the next posters of a shelf, the cast, the ratings) snapped the page back to the highlighted section. Now the page stays where the pointer took it.
- From fork.8: **The edge scrolling stops on the top bar's buttons.** With the pointer on the profile button or on Home / Movies / Watchlist / Search the page no longer glides; move off them (still near the edge) and it glides on.
- From fork.7: **the posters peeking at the bottom of Home are clickable.** On the top of Home the first shelf shows under the big picture; clicking one of its posters now scrolls the page down to it (a second click opens it). The same goes for a poster cut off by the bottom edge further down. This was upstream behaviour (nothing there could be clicked until you scrolled).
- The Magic Remote pointer scrolling from fork.6:

- **Rest the pointer near the top or bottom edge and the page glides smoothly**, on Home and on the movie lists alike, whether the pointer is over a card or not. It eases in after a moment and moves faster the closer the pointer is to the edge. On Home it slides from the big hero picture into the shelves and back. Over the navigation bar at the top the pointer works the bar as usual.
- **Pointing at a card no longer moves the page.** It only highlights the card; partly shown cards stay where they are.
- **Clicking a card opens it, as before**; on Home a card only partly on screen is scrolled into view by the first click.
- **The arrow keys move a whole row at a time**, as before.

Applies to Home, libraries, Collections, the Watchlist, collections, people, Search and detail pages.

## This fork's features

- **Skip intros** / **Skip credits**: *Show button* or *Skip automatically*, with an "Intro skipped" / "Credits skipped" notice; per profile.
- **BACK hides the controls** is upstream's own, and so is leaving a video that will not start; the fork adds that Magic Remote jitter does not bring controls hidden with BACK straight back.
- **Media line**, e.g. `4K · Dolby Vision · HEVC · TrueHD Atmos 7.1`.

Not verified on a television yet: host unit tests and the ARM cross-build only.

## Installing

**Homebrew Channel (recommended, gets updates):** in Homebrew Channel open Settings, Repositories, and add

    https://raw.githubusercontent.com/danzig666/plx-native/homebrew/repo.json

then install **PlxNative** from that list. It replaces an installed PlxNative and keeps its sign-in. From this release on, fork builds are numbered `<major>.<minor>.<fork number>` (this one is **0.9.13**), so the Channel offers each new fork release as an update. A newer upstream release in webosbrew's own listing with a higher number (0.9.14 and up, or 0.10) will be offered as an update too, and would replace the fork.

**By hand:**

- `com.beb.plxnative_0.9.13_arm.ipk` installs **over** an existing PlxNative and keeps its sign-in.
- `com.beb.plxnative.debug_0.9.13_arm.ipk` installs **beside** it as a separate tile with its own sign-in (and a developer frame counter).

Install with webOS Dev Manager like any `.ipk`. Checksums are in `SHA256SUMS.txt`. The stable package reports no telemetry: no telemetry endpoint is built in.

The bundled FFmpeg is built by the same `ci/build-ffmpeg.sh` as upstream. Its LGPL corresponding source is the source asset on the [upstream v0.9.0 release](https://github.com/GLinnik21/plx-native/releases/tag/v0.9.0).
